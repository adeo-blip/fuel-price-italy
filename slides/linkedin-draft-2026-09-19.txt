Fuel Dashboard is an interactive, self-updating view of Italian fuel prices by region, province, municipality, operator and brand, built on the official MIMIT open data. Every day it refreshes itself, picks a region to spotlight, and writes a full report with trend analysis for petrol, diesel, LPG and methane. Take a look: https://adeo-blip.github.io/fuel-price-italy/

The point is not just the fuel prices. This whole pipeline, from data download to weekly trend analysis to the published dashboard and this report, was built with Claude Code and runs unattended. The same job in a manual Excel workbook would mean re-downloading, re-pasting, rebuilding charts and rewriting commentary every single day, and it would go stale the first day someone skipped it.
