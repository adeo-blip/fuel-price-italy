Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight region is Lombardia — and the most interesting number isn't today's price, it's the trend underneath it.

Since late July, methane (Metano) prices have climbed everywhere in Italy, but not at the same pace: nationally they're up 37.5%, while in Lombardia they've risen a more modest 26.4%. That's widened Lombardia's discount to the national Metano average from about 2% in July to nearly 10% today (€1.9056/L in Lombardia vs €2.1022/L nationally, as of Nov 7). Benzina and Gasolio tell a calmer story — both rose into a September peak and have since eased back to roughly 2-4% above where they started in July, in Lombardia and nationally alike.
