Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight: Liguria.

Self-service Benzina and Gasolio in Liguria are both running slightly below the national average right now (-0.5% and -0.4%), but the real story this week is gas. Liguria's Metano price has climbed almost 39% since late July, well ahead of the roughly 25% rise seen nationally over the same twelve weeks, and GPL has pulled 15% above the national average too, rising more than three times as fast as the rest of Italy since tracking began.

Fuel Dashboard is a live, self-updating site that pulls Italy's official MIMIT fuel price feed every day, picks a region to spotlight, computes the week-over-week trends, and writes up the findings, all automatically. No spreadsheet, no manual pivot tables, no analyst re-pasting CSVs each morning: the whole pipeline, including this write-up, runs on Claude Code.

That's the real pitch here: a one-person side project can now ship something that would have taken a small analytics team to maintain as a manual Excel tracker, not because the data is secret, but because AI tools make daily ingestion, analysis and writing cheap enough to automate end to end.
