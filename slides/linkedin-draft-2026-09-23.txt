Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight: Calabria.

The most interesting number isn't today's price — it's the trend. Calabria's Metano (natural gas) has carried a large premium over the national average all season, but that gap has been quietly closing: from roughly 21% above the rest of Italy in late July to under 15% today, even as Calabria's own metano price kept climbing 12.7% over the same ten weeks. Benzina and Gasolio, meanwhile, are tracking the national curve almost tick for tick, and GPL hasn't moved at all in Calabria since tracking began in July.
