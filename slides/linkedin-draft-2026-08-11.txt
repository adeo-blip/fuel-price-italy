Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight falls on Veneto, which has quietly slipped under a psychological line: self-service petrol at €1.9953 per litre on August 10th, below both the two-euro mark and the national average of €1.9981. Diesel is a touch friendlier still — €2.0984 regionally against €2.1071 nationally.

It is the sixth consecutive day of falling prices in the region, off a peak on August 4th. But the moves are getting smaller: less than a tenth of a cent between yesterday and today, which usually means a slide running out of road rather than a trend building.

The contrarian number is methane. While petrol drifted down, Veneto's Metano climbed 6.86 cents since July 21st to €1.6162 per kg — over 5 cents above the national figure. Two fuels, same region, opposite directions, same three weeks.

One more sign of a live network: 1,836 of Veneto's 1,997 registered stations reported a price this morning, 91.9% against 90.5% nationally.
