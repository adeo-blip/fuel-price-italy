Fuel Dashboard is a free, self-updating interactive dashboard that tracks self-service and full-service prices for Benzina, Gasolio, GPL and Metano across every Italian region, province, municipality, and fuel brand — updated daily, with a different region getting a deep-dive report each day. Today's ~10-page report on Molise, with the full weekly trend breakdown behind these numbers, is live now: https://adeo-blip.github.io/fuel-price-italy/

The part I think is genuinely interesting: nothing about this pipeline is manually maintained. There's no spreadsheet someone updates every morning, no analyst building weekly averages by hand, no one drafting the write-up. I built the entire thing — the data pipeline, the trend analysis, the region rotation, and the daily report generation — with Claude Code, and it runs unattended every single day.
