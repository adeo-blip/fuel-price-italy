Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight: Lombardia. The region holds a discount on every fuel versus the rest of Italy, but the most interesting number isn't today's price — it's how fast that gap is moving. Lombardia's Metano price has climbed 5.44% in just the last three tracked weeks, nearly three times the national rate, closing its discount from almost 10% to under 7% in that span. GPL shows the same pattern over the full four months tracked: Lombardia's price rose 14.66% since July versus 9.81% nationally, nearly erasing what was a 6% regional discount.
