Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight lands on Trentino-Alto Adige, and the standout finding isn't the daily price — it's a nine-week trend. The region has long carried Italy's steepest premium on Metano (natural gas for vehicles), but that gap has shrunk from roughly 20% to about 12.5% since late July, simply because national methane prices are rising almost twice as fast as the region's own. Petrol and diesel, meanwhile, are moving in near-lockstep with the national market, and LPG is one of the only fuels bucking the trend by rising locally while easing nationally.
