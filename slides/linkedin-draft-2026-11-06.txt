Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight: Lazio.

The number that stood out: Lazio's Metano has historically cost more than the Italian average — as much as 7% more back in July. This week, for the first time since I started tracking, that premium flipped: the national average (€2.049/L) edged above Lazio's (€2.047/L). Benzina and Gasolio barely moved day-over-day, while GPL quietly became the region's fastest-rising fuel, up 3.6% in the past month alone.
