Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight lands on Marche, and the standout story isn't the daily price move — it's what happens when you zoom out. Self-service Metano in the region has climbed 22.82% since tracking began in late July, outpacing the 19.33% national increase over the same ten weeks, the widest gap between a region and the national trend of any fuel we track. GPL tells a quieter version of the same story: Marche sits 19% above the national LPG average, and that gap has been widening, not closing, even as the rest of Italy's GPL prices eased slightly. Meanwhile Gasolio just posted its sharpest single-day pullback of the tracked period, down 2.51% after setting its highest weekly average since the tracker began.
