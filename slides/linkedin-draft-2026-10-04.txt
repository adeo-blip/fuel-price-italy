Today's spotlight is Sardegna. Its self-service petrol and diesel have each risen about 6.5% since late July, slightly faster than the national rise of about 6.2%, and both now sit a fraction above the Italian average.

The most striking number is metano. Nationally, self-service metano climbed 20.6% over the tracked weeks, the steepest rise of the four fuels, yet the regional series carries no self-service metano price at all. The national trend is real, but this region's data cannot show it.

Fuel Dashboard tracks Italian fuel prices daily from official MIMIT data and turns them into an interactive dashboard and a written report. Explore it at https://adeo-blip.github.io/fuel-price-italy/

All of it runs unattended every morning: the data pull, the weekly analysis, the rotating region and the write-up. It was built with Claude Code, and it makes the case for AI tools over a manually maintained Excel sheet, which only stays current as long as someone remembers to update it.
