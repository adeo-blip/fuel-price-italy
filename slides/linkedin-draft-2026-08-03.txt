Fuel Dashboard is a free, self-updating site tracking petrol and diesel prices across all 20 Italian regions, refreshed every morning straight from the MIMIT national feed. No spreadsheet, no manual re-entry — just a pipeline that pulls the data, picks a different region to spotlight each day, and republishes itself automatically. Take a look: https://adeo-blip.github.io/fuel-price-italy/

The part I keep coming back to: this entire thing — data ingestion, daily region rotation, article writing, site rebuild — runs end-to-end with Claude Code.
