Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight is on Puglia, and the most interesting number isn't the daily price — it's the trend underneath it. Over the past eight weeks, Italy's national Metano price has actually risen faster (+13.2%) than Puglia's own already-elevated price (+8.5%), meaning the rest of the country is catching up to what Puglia has been paying at the pump for methane. Meanwhile Puglia's GPL, already the region's cheapest fuel by a wide margin, has kept drifting down relative to the national average — a 6.5% discount that's still widening.

Fuel Dashboard tracks Benzina, Gasolio, GPL, and Metano prices across all 20 Italian regions, every provincia, and thousands of comuni, refreshed daily from Italy's official MIMIT open-data feed. You can explore it live at https://adeo-blip.github.io/fuel-price-italy/.

What I think is worth calling out: this entire dashboard, the daily data pipeline, the region-of-the-day report, and even the analysis paragraphs above were built and are run end-to-end with Claude Code — no manually-maintained Excel sheet, no person recalculating weekly averages by hand. It updates itself every morning. That's the case for using AI coding tools for this kind of ongoing data project: the marginal cost of covering one more region, one more fuel, or one more day is close to zero once the pipeline exists, which just isn't true of a spreadsheet someone has to babysit.
