Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight lands on Abruzzo, and the striking thing is how unremarkable it is: self-service petrol at €1.9944 per litre on August 11th, just 0.16 cents below the national €1.9960. Diesel is closer still and on the wrong side — €2.1049 regionally against €2.1044 nationally, a gap of five hundredths of a cent. If you wanted a region that simply is the Italian average, this is it.

Petrol has now fallen for eight consecutive days, off a €2.0097 peak on August 4th, dropping back under two euros on the 8th. But the momentum is gone: less than a tenth of a cent of movement between yesterday and today on both petrol and diesel.

The number that actually moved is methane. Abruzzo's Metano jumped 11.11 cents across two days in early August, to €1.5154 per kg — and has then printed exactly the same figure for six straight days. It is still the region's one real discount, 5.20 cents below the national average.
