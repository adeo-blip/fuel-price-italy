Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight is Toscana, and it produced an unusually clean number: self-service Benzina at €2.0065, which is exactly the national self-service average, matching to the fourth decimal place. Diesel is barely distinguishable too, €2.1205 regionally against €2.1199 nationally.

Underneath that stillness there is movement. Self-service petrol in Toscana has fallen for three days running, down 0.39 cents from its August 4th peak, with diesel off 0.63 cents over the same stretch. Metano went the other way entirely: up 1.67 cents in twenty-four hours to €1.6140, now almost six cents above the national average. And the region does break away on full service, €2.1794 versus €2.1459 nationally. Of 1,648 registered stations in Toscana, 1,490 reported prices today.

These figures come from Fuel Dashboard, a project that tracks Italian fuel prices daily from MIMIT open data — national and regional averages across Benzina, Gasolio, GPL and Metano, self and full service, with day-by-day history and a different region in the spotlight every morning.
