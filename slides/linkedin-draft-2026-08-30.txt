Fuel Dashboard tracks fuel prices across all 23,973 registered stations in Italy, updates itself every day straight from the MIMIT open data feed, and rotates through a different region each day for a closer look — no manual spreadsheet, no stale numbers. Check today's numbers and the rest of the country at https://adeo-blip.github.io/fuel-price-italy/

This is also a small case study in what's now possible with AI coding tools: the entire pipeline, from data ingestion to the daily regional write-up you're reading, was built and is maintained with Claude Code.
