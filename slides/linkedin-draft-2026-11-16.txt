Fuel Dashboard is a free, self-updating interactive dashboard that tracks petrol, diesel, LPG and methane prices across every region, province, municipality and operator in Italy, refreshed daily from the Ministry's own MIMIT feed. No one opens a spreadsheet to do this — a small unattended pipeline built with Claude Code pulls the data, rotates through Italy's 20 regions for a daily spotlight, computes the weekly trends, and writes the report you're reading right now, every single day without manual intervention.

That's the real pitch here: a few years ago this would have meant a manually-maintained Excel file, updated whenever someone remembered to. Today it's a live interactive site plus a fresh, data-grounded written report, generated end-to-end by AI tooling.
