Today's Fuel Dashboard spotlight: Lombardia, where self-service diesel just reversed a full month of gains — dropping over 12 cents a liter in three days — while gasoline kept its slow, steady climb toward the national average.

Fuel Dashboard tracks daily fuel prices across all of Italy's regions, pulling directly from the official MIMIT feed and rotating a regional spotlight every day. No manual spreadsheet, no stale numbers — just today's real figures, every day. Take a look: https://adeo-blip.github.io/fuel-price-italy/

What I like most about this project: the entire thing, from data pipeline to daily regional articles, was built with Claude Code instead of a manually maintained Excel file. It's a good example of what AI coding tools are actually good for right now — not just writing code faster, but building small systems that stay current on their own, so nobody has to remember to update anything.
